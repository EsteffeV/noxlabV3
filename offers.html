Our offers

From interface audit to neuroscience study, every offer ends with prioritised recommendations.

All our offers are quoted. The starting point is a 30-minute call, then a scoping note: your question, the state of research, what we propose, a quote.

Tell us about your project

01

Human factors audit of an interface

On a busy screen, a critical piece of information can go unnoticed.

We find what gets lost on your screen and quantify the visibility of every critical element.

For whom
Medical device manufacturers, publishers of software and critical interfaces (supervision, cockpit, dashboard), before a user test or a redesign.
You receive
An audit report based on reference ergonomic criteria and the applicable standards (IEC 62366-1, IEC 60073), the visibility of each critical element quantified by a visual attention model, prioritised recommendations and corrective mock-ups.
Lead time
1 to 2 weeks

For a medical device, the audit fits into formative evaluation and feeds the usability engineering file (IEC 62366-1).

Example: alarms in intensive care

02

Scientific review and recommendations

Making the case for a product, choosing a method or a sensor in the light of the scientific literature.

We establish what research shows, and what it does not, to support your decision.

For whom
Teams who must choose a sensor, an indicator or a protocol, or base a product decision on the state of research.
You receive
A synthesis of what research shows and what it does not, verified and annotated references, recommendations that apply to your product.
Lead time
1 to 2 weeks

03

Study with participants

You need to measure your product’s effect on the people who use it, beyond what they say about it.

We test your product with participants, in its conditions of use: physiological and behavioural measures, and questionnaires.

For whom
Teams who must validate a product or an interface in its conditions of use, in the lab, on a simulator or in the field, with participants alone or in teams.
You receive
The protocol, recruitment and sessions, the measurements (eye, cardiac, respiratory and electrodermal signals, EEG, questionnaires), statistical analysis, the report and the debrief.
Lead time
On quotation, depending on the protocol

04

Analysis of existing data

You have data, but you don’t know how to make use of it.

We turn your data into quantified results, with their limits, ready to present.

For whom
Teams who already have data (sensors, user tests, trials) and want to know what it shows.
You receive
Signal processing, statistics, quantified results with their limits, presentation-ready figures, recommendations.
Lead time
1 to 3 weeks

Example: driver fatigue

What we measure

What your users experience, not just what they report.

We start from your question, then choose the most suitable measure.

  • Where does their gaze go?

    Eye signals (eye tracking)

  • How heavy is the mental workload?

    Questionnaires, EEG, fNIRS, cardiac signals

  • How stressed are they?

    Questionnaires, electrodermal and cardiac signals

  • What emotional reaction?

    Questionnaires, electrodermal signals

  • Which gestures, which posture?

    Motion capture

A 30-minute call, then a scoping note within 2 days.
