Privacy policy

Data controllers

Zeynep Yenigün and Esteffe Violet, under the trade name Nox Science, 15 rue Labat, 75018 Paris, France. Contact: contact@noxscience.com.

Data we process, why and for how long

  • Contact form: name, company, e-mail and message. To answer your request.
  • E-mails sent to or from contact@noxscience.com. To correspond with you.

These uses rest on your own request (steps taken before a contract, at your request) and on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. We keep this data for 3 years after our last contact, then delete it.

Technical connection logs (including your IP address) are processed by our host to serve and protect the site, under its own retention period.

What this site does not do

No advertising, no audience measurement, no profiling. Typefaces are hosted on the site: your browser contacts no third-party service to display them. Your language choice (FR or EN) is remembered in your browser only: it contains no personal data and is never sent to us.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare (United States): hosting and delivery of the site.
  • Formspree (United States): receiving contact form messages.
  • OVHcloud (France): mail service for contact@noxscience.com.

Where these providers are outside the European Union, the transfer relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction, erasure, restriction or objection to its processing, and its portability, by writing to contact@noxscience.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Study participants

Study participants' data never passes through this site. It is handled separately, with information specific to each study.
