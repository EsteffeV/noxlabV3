Case studies

A few examples of our work.

Interface ergonomics · November 2026

Assessing alarm visibility on a medical interface

Our ergonomic audit and a visual attention model reveal that with several alarms, the most urgent one became the least visible, which increased the risk of error. Our improvement makes it 2.2 times more visible.

Read the case study

Data analysis · October 2026

Measuring driver fatigue

Measuring and predicting fatigue at the wheel could prevent 10 to 24% of crashes. To choose a reliable sensor, we compared several multimodal signals with a reference: a sleep physician’s rating based on electroencephalography (EEG).

Read the case study

Emotion measurement · October 2026

Assessing the emotional impact of an environment

Does a virtual reality scene designed to produce joy actually produce that emotion? With emotion questionnaires, psychophysiological and brain measures, we showed that it did not.
