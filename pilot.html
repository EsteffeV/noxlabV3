Human factors

Understanding how your users really use your product

Human factors is the study of how people behave with a product or a system. It lets you:

  • study the behaviour of your users;
  • analyse use risks;
  • optimise performance and comfort;
  • adapt the product to people: ergonomics;
  • measure the body’s response (fatigue, workload, stress), beyond what people say.

What we offer

8 to 10 days of work, on one precise question

Fatigue & vigilance

Drowsiness and drops in attention at the wheel or at work, tracked second by second.

Comfort & posture

Products, spaces and everyday objects: how comfort changes over time, through posture, physiological signals and what people report.

Sound & ambience

Sounds, scents and ambiences compared on the body's response, not only on what people say.

Data analysis

Whether we record it or you already have it: we analyse the data and tell you what it shows.

User tests and field measurement

Have representative users handle your prototype or your sensor, spot use errors and propose fixes. Assess reliability, comfort and acceptability in real conditions.

Use risks of a medical device

Analyse the tasks and possible errors on a medical device, following the approach expected for CE marking (IEC 62366-1).

We adapt to your product, your equipment and your data, or we bring our own measurement equipment. You leave with a tailored protocol, a short report with prioritised recommendations and the raw data, and an oral debrief with your team.

Who it is for

The programme is for companies that…

  • develop a product where the user matters: health, connected objects, sport, well-being, or any other sector;
  • have a product or prototype that can be tested in the coming weeks;
  • name a contact available at least 1 hour a week;
  • agree that we communicate about the collaboration, under the conditions defined together.

We work on site in Paris and Lille, and remotely elsewhere when the mission allows.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is it offered?

We are launching Nox Science and building our first case studies with companies whose products interest us. In exchange, we ask to be able to communicate about the collaboration.

How will you communicate about the collaboration?

We want to communicate about our collaboration, while respecting confidentiality. You have the right to review and to veto any content, and everything is set out in a contract.

Does my information stay confidential?

Yes. Non-public information is covered by a confidentiality clause (NDA) signed before the pilot starts, and the data of test participants is anonymised.

How much time does it take from my team?

At least 1 hour a week of exchange with one person. Access to the product and to users, where applicable.

What equipment do you use?

We can adapt to your product and your sensors, or use our own sensors and equipment. The protocol is defined together during the first call.

Where do you work?

On site in Paris and Lille; elsewhere, remotely when the mission allows.

Is the pilot enough for my CE marking?

No. It can feed your process, but it does not replace the final validation of your file.

What if we want to go further?

We can continue beyond the pilot, as a standard engagement.
